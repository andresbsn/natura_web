# ADR-005: Brand Usage And Official Integrations

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The project is for a Natura reseller, not an official Natura ecommerce platform. Product information, images, logos and possible endpoints may be owned or controlled by Natura.

## Decision
The application may use a natural/cosmetics-inspired visual style, but should avoid copying official brand identity unless authorized. Official product/API integration is allowed only if Natura provides documented public access or formal authorization.

## Consequences
- Initial product loading will be manual, with possible CSV/Excel import later.
- Avoid dependence on private or reverse-engineered Natura endpoints.
- Legal/brand approval may be needed before using official logos, images or automated product feeds.
